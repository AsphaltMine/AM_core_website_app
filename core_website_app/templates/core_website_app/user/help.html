Getting Started

What is the {{CUSTOM_NAME}}?

{{CUSTOM_NAME}} is a platform for collecting asphalt test results in an organized online database and for using this data to train machine learning models to predict asphalt performance. More information can be found here.

What tasks can I perform on the {{CUSTOM_NAME}}?

A user can input and curate asphalt test results, search and explore existing data, display or conceal their data, and share it either publicly or with specific groups.

What asphalt tests are available in the {{CUSTOM_NAME}}?

The available tests in the {{CUSTOM_NAME}} are:

  • Rutting Test
  • Marshall Test
  • Indirect Tensile Strength Test (ITS)
  • Thermal Stress Restrained Specimen Test (TSRST)
  • Uniaxial Tension Stress Test (UTST)
  • Stiffness Test

You can view the data structure for these tests here.

Do I need an account to use the {{CUSTOM_NAME}}?

Visualization, Prediction and this FAQ open without an account. Searching the database, entering data, uploading data and using workspaces need you to be logged in.

Accounts & Access

How do I get access to the {{CUSTOM_NAME}}?
  1. Click Log In / Sign Up in the top menu and click Create Account.
  2. Fill in the required fields and captcha on the Create Account page, then click Create Account.
  3. Open the verification email sent to your address and click the link inside to activate your account.

Your account is now active, with read-only access. To also enter or upload data, request write access (see below).

How do I create an account?
  1. Click Log In / Sign Up in the top menu.
  2. On the login page, click Create Account.
  3. Fill in the required fields marked with a red asterisk, such as your username, first name, last name, email address, password, password confirmation, and the captcha.
  4. Click Create Account.
  5. Open the email sent to your address and click the verification link to activate your account.
  6. Click Log In on the Email Verified page to sign in.
I did not receive the verification email. What can I do?
  1. Check your email spam or junk folder to see if the message arrived there.
  2. If it is not there, click Didn't get it? Resend the email on the Check Your Email page, or go to the login page and click Resend the verification email.
  3. On the Resend Verification Email page, type the email address you signed up with, complete the captcha, and click Resend Email.
I forgot my password. How do I reset it?
  1. Click Log In / Sign Up at the top right of the page.
  2. On the login page, click Forgot password.
  3. Type your email address and click Send reset link.
  4. Open the email you receive and click the link inside it.
  5. Enter your new password and click Change password.
  6. Click Sign in to use your new password.
What can I do with a read-only account?

With a read-only account you can log in, search and view records in the database, and use the Visualization and Prediction pages.

How do I request write access?
  1. Open My Account in the top menu and click My Profile.
  2. Find the Write Access row and click Request Write Access.
  3. Fill in the form with your organization name, source country or countries, standards followed, and a short description.
  4. Click Send Request.

You will receive an email once the {{CUSTOM_NAME}} team reviews your request.

What happens after I send a write access request?
  • If your request is accepted, you will receive an email confirming that write access has been granted.
  • If it is denied, your account remains read-only and you can send a new request.

Data Curation

How do I enter a new test record?
  1. Log in, then click Data Curation in the top menu. Write access is also required.
  2. Click the box under Performance Tests on the right to select the test or tests you want.
  3. Click Apply.
  4. Open each section by clicking its Show tab and fill in the required fields, marked with a red asterisk.
  5. Click Save at the bottom of the page.
  6. In the confirmation box, choose whether to keep the record private or assign it to a workspace, and click Confirm.
Can I enter results for more than one test at once?

Yes.

  1. Go to the Data Curation page in the top menu.
  2. Click the box under Performance Tests on the right side of the screen.
  3. In the popup that appears, tick all the tests you want to include and click Apply.
  4. Fill in the shared mixture and data source details, along with the sample properties and results sections for each of the selected tests.
  5. Click Save at the bottom of the page to save all the records at once.

This needs write access.

Why can't I save my record?

Usually one of two reasons: a required field is empty or formatted incorrectly, or your account does not have write access.

  • If a required field is missing, the browser highlights it in red when you click Save.
  • If your account has read-only access, the action fails with an "Insufficient Permission" message. You can request write access from My Profile.
How do I edit a record I already saved?
  1. Open the Database page or My Data page. Logging in is required.
  2. On the Database page, find your record and click the pencil icon next to it.
  3. Or, on the My Data page, open the actions menu for your record and click Edit.
  4. Make your changes in the Data Curation form that opens.
  5. Click Update at the bottom of the page, then click Confirm in the confirmation box.

Only the owner can edit a record, and the test type cannot be changed after saving.

What does "semi-mandatory" mean for a field?

The form allows you to save a record without filling in that field, but a useful record should include at least one entry for each semi-mandatory group:

  • Grain size distribution: Virgin Aggregates or Recovered Aggregates.
  • Binder content: Binder Content in Mixture Recipe or Percentage Content of Recovered Binder.
  • Penetration: Virgin Binder Penetration or Recovered Binder Penetration.
  • Voids: Bulk density with Maximum density, or Air voids.
How do I download a single record as a file?
  1. Open the record in the Data Curation page (find it through the Database or My Data pages).
  2. Click the Download button at the bottom of the form.
  3. Click Confirm in the popup box to save the record to your device in XML format.

Bulk Upload

How do I upload many records at once?

Use the Bulk Upload page. This needs login and curate (write) access.

  1. Click Bulk Upload in the top navigation menu.
  2. Download the predefined Excel template using the link provided on the page.
  3. Fill in your data in the Excel file.
  4. Click Upload AsphaltMine Excel File and select your completed file.
  5. Choose the correct sheet layout, Database (Columns) or Database (Rows), that matches your file.
  6. Click Extract, then Confirm.
  7. Once extraction is successful, click Validate, then Confirm.
  8. If all records are valid, choose a workspace if desired.
  9. Click Save, then Confirm, to add them to the database.

If any records are invalid, the system lists them; correct your Excel file and repeat from step 4.

Where do I get the Excel template?
  1. Click Bulk Upload in the top navigation menu.
  2. Look in the About this page box and click the link named here to download the template file.

Alternatively, download any existing record from the Database page or My Data using the AsphaltMine Format (.xlsx) download option, which is also fully compatible with Bulk Upload.

Is there a file size limit for Bulk Upload?

Yes, the server rejects uploaded files larger than about 10 MB. If your Excel file is bigger, split it into several smaller files and upload them one after another.

What does "INVALID: Missing field" mean?

A required value is empty in that row or column of your Excel sheet. Fill in the missing value in your sheet, upload the file again, and run the extraction and validation steps once more.

Can one row in my Excel sheet create more than one record?

Yes, if it contains results for more than one test. {{CUSTOM_NAME}} creates one separate record for each test that has results in that row.

Database & Search

How do I search for records?
  1. Click Database in the top menu.
  2. Click the search box at the top of the page.
  3. Type a word and press Enter or the space bar to turn it into a keyword tag.
  4. Wait about three seconds for the search to run automatically, or click the magnifying glass button to run it right away.
What download formats are available?

The download formats available on the Database page are Simple Export (.xlsx), Simple Export (.csv), and AsphaltMine Format (.xlsx). The AsphaltMine Format is compatible with Bulk Upload but much slower to prepare, and can take tens of minutes for downloads with thousands of records.

How do I download every record I am allowed to see?
  1. Click Database in the top navigation menu.
  2. Leave the search box completely blank so every record you are allowed to see is listed.
  3. Click Select All to select every matching record across all pages.
  4. Click Download.
  5. In the download options box, choose your preferred format and click Download again.

My Data & Workspaces

Who can see the data I upload?

Your uploaded data is private to you by default and is visible only to you. Other people can see it only if you move the records into a shared workspace or a public workspace.

How do I create a workspace?
  1. Click Shared Workspaces in the top menu. A login is required.
  2. Click Create workspace.
  3. In the Create new workspace box, type a name in Workspace name.
  4. Click Create Workspace.

This needs write access. Read-only accounts cannot create a workspace.

Can I give someone write access without making my workspace public?

Yes.

  1. Click Shared Workspaces in the top menu.
  2. Find your workspace in the table and open its Actions menu.
  3. Click Manage Access.
  4. Click Add users and search for the person in the box to add them.
  5. Tick Write access next to their name so they can add or edit records without the workspace being public.
  6. Click Add.
How do I move several records into a workspace at once?
  1. Click My Data in the top menu. This requires logging in.
  2. Click Modify Records. Checkboxes appear next to your records, and the button changes to Done.
  3. Tick the checkboxes for the records you want to move, or tick the box in the table header to select every record matching your current filters across all pages.
  4. Open the Choose action menu and pick Move selected records to a workspace.
  5. Choose your target workspace and confirm.
  6. Click Done to leave this mode.
Can I change who owns a record?

Yes, if you own the record.

  1. Click My Data in the top menu to open your records list. Logging in is required.
  2. Find the record and click the sliders icon in the Actions column for that record.
  3. Select Change Owner.
  4. In the box that appears, choose the new owner from the list of active users and click Change.

Visualization

What is Live Insights?

Live Insights shows live summaries of the curated records in the database. You can find it on the Visualization page under the Live Insights tab. It updates automatically every hour, or manually with Refresh once a minute if you are logged in.

Can I see statistics for just my own workspace?

Yes, by switching the scope on the Live Insights tab. You must be logged in to use this feature.

What is the correlation matrix?

In the drill-down view for each test, it shows how strongly pairs of parameters relate to one another.

  • The lower triangle uses the Pearson coefficient, for linear relations.
  • The upper triangle uses the Spearman coefficient, for rank-based relations.

Each cell shows the coefficient and the number of paired records.

Prediction

Which predictions are available?

{{CUSTOM_NAME}} currently provides prediction models for the Marshall Test (predicting Marshall Stability and Flow) and for Volumetric Properties (predicting Bulk Density and Air Voids). Models for other tests are planned for the future.

How accurate is the Marshall prediction model?

Results are shown with ±σR and ±R ranges per EN 12697-34, representing the experimental reproducibility of the test. It was built using Physics-Informed Neural Networks and trained on 2,656 Swiss records, mostly asphalt concrete.

What data was the Marshall model trained on?

2,656 records from Switzerland, mainly from the cantons of Zurich and Aargau, consisting mostly of asphalt concrete. About 70% of the mixtures used polymer modified binders (mainly grades 45/80-80 and 45/80-65, all SBS modified), and about 30% used unmodified binders (mainly 50/70 and 70/100). All Marshall tests were run at 60°C, following EN 12697-34.

Can I download my prediction results?

Yes, click Download PDF Report below the results on the prediction page.

What is Ask {{CUSTOM_NAME}}?

Publications

What is the Publications page?

The Publications page lists the {{CUSTOM_NAME}} papers, which describe the platform and its prediction models, and the studies by other authors that used {{CUSTOM_NAME}} data or the prediction models. It is organized in three tabs: AsphaltMine Foundation, Asphalt Prediction Models and Studies Using AsphaltMine.

What do the three tabs on the Publications page contain?

The Publications page has three tabs that contain the following:

  1. AsphaltMine Foundation: The paper about the {{CUSTOM_NAME}} database itself.
  2. Asphalt Prediction Models: The papers that describe the prediction models, grouped under a heading for each model.
  3. Studies Using AsphaltMine: Publications by other authors that used {{CUSTOM_NAME}} data or prediction models, grouped by year with the newest year first.
How do I cite {{CUSTOM_NAME}} in my paper?
  1. Open the Publications page from the top menu.
  2. Select the AsphaltMine Foundation tab.
  3. Click the Cite button next to Read the paper.
  4. Choose Text to copy the reference, or RIS or BibTeX to download a file for a reference manager.

If you used the prediction models, cite the paper of the model and refer to {{CUSTOM_NAME}} in your paper.

If I used the prediction models, what do I have to cite?

Cite the paper of the model and refer to {{CUSTOM_NAME}} in your paper.

How can I search or filter the publications that used {{CUSTOM_NAME}}?
  1. Click Publications in the top menu.
  2. Click the Studies Using AsphaltMine tab.
  3. Use the Search papers box to type keywords from the title, authors, journal, year, or abstract, or use the Filter box to narrow results by usage, access, or type.
What do Used data and Used prediction models mean on a publication?

On the Studies Using AsphaltMine tab of the Publications page, these labels show which {{CUSTOM_NAME}} resources were used in that research. Used data means the authors used test records from the database, while Used prediction models means they used {{CUSTOM_NAME}}'s machine learning prediction tools.

Can I add my own publication that used {{CUSTOM_NAME}}?

Yes, if you are logged in and your work used {{CUSTOM_NAME}} data or the prediction models and cited {{CUSTOM_NAME}}. It must be a published journal article, conference paper or book chapter indexed in Scopus. Preprints are not accepted.

  1. Click Publications in the top menu.
  2. Open the Studies Using AsphaltMine tab.
  3. Click the Submit a publication button at the top of the tab.
  4. Enter the DOI of the publication in the box.
  5. Select the {{CUSTOM_NAME}} resources used in the publication (Used data, Used prediction models).
  6. Optionally add any additional information, then click Submit.
Why is the Submit a publication button disabled?

The Submit a publication button is disabled for two reasons:

  1. You are not logged in. Visitors who are not signed in see the button disabled.
  2. Even when you are logged in, the Submit button inside the submission window stays disabled until you enter a DOI that passes the automatic checks (the publication is found, it is a published journal article, conference paper or book chapter, and its journal is indexed in Scopus) and select at least one {{CUSTOM_NAME}} resource used in the publication (Used data or Used prediction models).

To use it, sign in to your account, open the Publications page, click the Studies Using AsphaltMine tab, and then click Submit a publication.

Which publications are accepted?

Scopus indexed journal articles, conference papers, and book chapters whose work used {{CUSTOM_NAME}} data or the prediction models and cited {{CUSTOM_NAME}} are accepted. Preprints are not accepted. Work that only cites an {{CUSTOM_NAME}} paper without using the data or models is not listed. The preprint, publication type and Scopus requirements are checked automatically when you enter the DOI, and a publication that does not meet them cannot be submitted.

Is my publication listed if it only cites an {{CUSTOM_NAME}} paper?

No. Citing an {{CUSTOM_NAME}} paper is not enough. The Studies Using AsphaltMine tab lists only publications whose work used {{CUSTOM_NAME}} data or the prediction models.

What does the automatic check do when I enter a DOI?

When you enter a DOI in the Submit a publication window, it checks it automatically and shows a tick or a cross for each of the following:

  1. Whether the publication is found
  2. Whether it is a published journal article, conference paper or book chapter
  3. Whether it cites an {{CUSTOM_NAME}} paper
  4. Whether its journal is indexed in Scopus

All checks except the citation check must pass before you can submit.

What happens after I submit a publication?

The {{CUSTOM_NAME}} team verifies that the work used {{CUSTOM_NAME}} data or the prediction models. Once verified, the publication appears on the Studies Using AsphaltMine tab.

Why is my publication not listed yet?

Your publication is not listed for one of these reasons:

  1. It is still in the verification process.
  2. The work did not use {{CUSTOM_NAME}} data or the prediction models.
  3. The work used {{CUSTOM_NAME}} data but did not cite the foundation paper.
  4. The work used the prediction models but did not cite the foundation paper, and did not cite the paper of the model together with a reference to {{CUSTOM_NAME}}.

Once verified, the publication appears on the Studies Using AsphaltMine tab.
