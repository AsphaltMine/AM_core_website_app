What is the {{CUSTOM_NAME}}?

{{CUSTOM_NAME}} is a platform for collecting asphalt test results in an organized online database and for using this data to train machine learning models to predict asphalt performance. More information can be found here.

How do I get access to the {{CUSTOM_NAME}}?

Anyone can create an account by clicking Log In / Sign Up and then Create Account. After you verify your email address, the account is active with read-only access. To enter or upload data, write to contact@asphaltmine.org to request write access.

What tasks can I perform on the {{CUSTOM_NAME}}?

A user can input and curate asphalt test results, search and explore existing data, display or conceal their data, and share it either publicly or with specific groups.

What asphalt tests are available in the {{CUSTOM_NAME}}?

Currently, the available tests in the {{CUSTOM_NAME}} are the Rutting Test, Marshall Test, Indirect Tensile Strength Test, Thermal Stress Restrained Specimen Test (TSRST), Uniaxial Tension Stress Test (UTST) and Stiffness Test. You can view the data structure for these tests here.

Do I need an account to use the {{CUSTOM_NAME}}?

You can open Visualization, Prediction and this FAQ without an account. Searching the database, entering data, uploading data and using workspaces need you to be logged in.

How do I create an account?

Click Log In / Sign Up, then Create Account. Fill in your username, first name, last name, email address and password, type the characters shown in the image, and click Create Account. A verification link is sent to your email address. Click it to activate your account.

I did not receive the verification email. What can I do?

Check your spam or junk folder first. If it is not there, click Didn't get it? Resend the email on the Check Your Email page, or Resend the verification email on the login page. Then type the email you signed up with, complete the captcha and click Resend Email.

I forgot my password. How do I reset it?

On the login page, click Forgot password, type your email and click Send reset link. Open the email, click the link and set a new password. The password needs at least 8 characters, including an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, a digit and a symbol.

What can I do with a read-only account?

A read-only account can log in, search and view records in the database, and use Visualization and Prediction. Saving records from Data Curation or Bulk Upload needs write access, which you can request at contact@asphaltmine.org.

Who can see the data I upload?

Your records are private to you by default. Other people can see them only if you move them into a workspace that is shared with specific users or groups, or that you set to public.

Which predictions are available?

The Marshall Test model predicts Marshall Stability and Flow, and the Volumetric Properties model predicts Bulk Density and Air Voids. Models for the other tests will be added over time. Prediction can be opened without an account.

What is Ask {{CUSTOM_NAME}}?

It is the chat button at the bottom right of the pages. The AI assistant answers questions about using {{CUSTOM_NAME}}, the tests, the data fields and the prediction models. It can make mistakes, so check important details with the team. Visitors who are not signed in can ask fewer questions per hour than signed in users. Your questions are sent to Google's Gemini AI service, so do not type passwords or confidential information.

How do I contact the {{CUSTOM_NAME}} team for questions?

You can contact the {{CUSTOM_NAME}} team by email at contact@asphaltmine.org.
